# Credits

## Backdrops

The four time-of-day backdrops in `res/drawable-nodpi/` are photographs of the Sahara
dunes in Morocco and Tunisia, taken from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to 16:9 and
graded (highlight roll-off, scrim, vignette) for use as a TV home backdrop.

| File | Original | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bg_morning.jpg` | [Merzouga 2024](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Merzouga_2024.jpg) | Jorge Franganillo | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `bg_afternoon.jpg` | [Sahara Sunrise, Morocco](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sahara_Sunrise,_Morocco_-_Flickr_-_Mustang_Joe.jpg) | Mustang Joe | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `bg_dusk.jpg` | [Watching the sunset in the Sahara, Morocco](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Watching_the_sunset_in_the_Sahara_-_Morocco.jpg) | Mustang Joe | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `bg_night.jpg` | [Night sky in the Sahara, Morocco](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Night_sky_in_the_Sahara_-_Morocco.jpg) | Mustang Joe | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

The graded derivatives in this repository are adaptations. `bg_morning.jpg` is
shared under the same terms as the original (CC BY 4.0); the other three are
public domain and carry no restrictions.
